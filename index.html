What MAPS means

Your doctor looks after the pregnancy. MAPS helps with what surrounds it.

A hospital booking, an insurance policy and money in the bank on the day of admission each answer one part of the question. MAPS puts them on the same page.

  1. M

    Membership

    A continuing relationship, and someone to turn to as the questions change.

  2. A

    Assistance

    Help finding ways to pay for care, and to manage when the bills fall due.

  3. P

    Protection

    Help understanding the financial risks around care and parenthood, early enough to act on them.

  4. S

    Sweeteners

    Rewards and benefits along the way, including MILA’S Miles.

Your doctor makes every medical decision. That does not change.

MILA’S Miles

How Miles work for the first hundred mothers.

Miles are rewards for steps taken in the programme. For the first hundred mothers, three steps completed within two weeks earn 8,000 Miles in all.

  1. 1Meet Mila and tell her about yourself3,000
  2. 2Attend the financial preparedness workshop3,000
  3. 3Complete the mental wellness check2,000

All three need to be completed within two weeks. Miles are not cash and do not replace insurance benefits.

Questions

What families usually ask first.

All questions
Is it free?

Building your map, talking to Mila and the walk-through of J Ward cost nothing. The first obstetrician consultation at St. Philomena’s is ₹500 and open to all. Care packages and sessions have prices, shown in full before you choose one. Mila explains what joining involves for your stage.

Does MILA’S replace my doctor?

No. Your doctor makes every medical decision, and your hospital provides your clinical care. MILA’S helps with what sits around that care, such as understanding costs and cover, preparing for appointments and knowing what comes next.

Is MILA’S a hospital, an insurer or a lender?

None of these. MILA’S does not diagnose or treat, and it does not underwrite insurance or give credit. Where a family needs a clinical service or a regulated financial one, it comes from the relevant authorised provider.

What does MAPS stand for?

Membership, Assistance, Protection and Sweeteners. Membership is the continuing relationship. Assistance and Protection deal with paying for care and the financial risks around it. Sweeteners are rewards such as MILA’S Miles.
