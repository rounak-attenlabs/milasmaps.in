For mothers still deciding

Come and see J Ward before you choose.

St. Philomena’s has cared for families in Bengaluru since 1937. The maternity ward is called J Ward. You can walk through it, meet the people who work there, and ask them anything, before you decide where your baby arrives.

J Ward

One package, one price, written down.

The delivery package at J Ward is one amount for the delivery and the standard stay, ₹1,56,000.

What it includes, what it leaves out, and what a longer stay or a different room costs are on the care packages page. We go through it with you line by line before you pay a deposit, and we tell you what your insurer is likely to say about the room.

The doctors

Your doctor stays your doctor.

The obstetricians at St. Philomena’s make the medical decisions with you. MILA’S never does. If you do not have a doctor yet, we help you meet one here before your next scan.

Delivery day

What the day looks like.

  1. Before. Pre-registration and the deposit are done in the due month, not on the day. Your file, your scans and your insurer’s pre-authorisation are already with the ward.
  2. Arriving.
  3. The stay.
  4. Going home. The bill is the package plus anything outside it, which you saw in writing before admission. The paediatrician books the first visits before you leave.

Mila is one message away on every one of these days, for the questions that are not medical.

Visiting

Who can come, and when.

Visiting hours and who may stay overnight depend on the room. Ask Mila for the current rules before the day.

Book a visit

Walk through J Ward. Ask the people how they decide.

Come and see the ward, meet the nurses and, if they are free, a doctor. Ask them how they decide when to keep going and when to act. A visit commits you to nothing. Most families say it made the choice simpler than reading about ten hospitals.
