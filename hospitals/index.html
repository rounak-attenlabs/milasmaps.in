Premium with purpose

A considered experience, inside a mission hospital.

A good childbirth experience is about more than an expensive room. It is confidence in your care, clear information about your choices and people who make you feel looked after.

MILA’S does not replace your doctor or make clinical decisions for you. It helps you understand what each hospital offers, prepare for conversations with your care team and handle the practical side of your care.

Clinical care, medical advice, treatment decisions and hospital charges remain the responsibility of the hospital. Services and benefits vary by hospital.
